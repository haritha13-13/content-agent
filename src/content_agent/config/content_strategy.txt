Speedbots Content Strategy

Speedbots is an AI automation company serving Malaysia, Singapore, Australia, and Coimbatore (Tamil Nadu, India).
Our services include AI automation, chatbots, AI agents, and workflow automation.

TARGET MARKETS:
- Malaysia: currency MYR, tax system SST, popular tools Xero/QuickBooks/MYOB
- Singapore: currency SGD, tax system GST (9%), popular tools Xero/QuickBooks/SAP
- Australia: currency AUD, tax system BAS/GST (10%), popular tools Xero/QuickBooks/MYOB
- Coimbatore: currency INR, tax system GST, popular tools Tally/Zoho Books

CORE CONTENT MODEL:
We build topic clusters, not isolated pages. The hierarchy is:
Service Hubs (4) -> Country Pages (8) -> Industry Pages (10) -> Solution Pages (10)
Every article links back to its parent hub page.

SERVICE HUBS:
- /ai-automation
- /ai-chatbot
- /accounting-automation
- /whatsapp-automation

SOLUTION PAGES:
- /ai-invoice-processing
- /ai-reconciliation
- /ai-appointment-booking
- /ai-customer-support
- /ai-document-processing

ARTICLE FORMULA (9 STEPS):
1. Direct answer (80-120 words) - define topic, include keyword, state value proposition
2. Business problem (300-500 words) - describe 3-4 manual pain points with specific numbers
3. Real-world workflows (400-600 words) - before/after automation flows with metrics
4. Implementation steps (300-500 words) - 6 practical steps starting with audit
5. Cost factors (200-400 words) - tables for what you pay vs what you save
6. Risks and controls (200-400 words) - 5 risks with mitigations (build trust)
7. Case example (200-300 words) - realistic company with measurable results
8. FAQ (300-500 words) - 6-8 questions with specific answers
9. Specific CTA (50-100 words) - book workflow assessment, never generic "contact us"

TOTAL WORD COUNT: 2000-3000 words

SEO RULES:
- Title format: "Primary Keyword | Secondary Keyword | Speedbots" (max 60 chars)
- Meta description: "Speedbots provides X, Y, and Z to streamline operations across Country1, Country2, Country3" (max 160 chars)
- Keyword density: primary 1-2%, secondary 0.5-1%
- Internal links: 2-5 per post, must link to parent cluster page
- FAQ schema: 8 questions
- Exactly one H1 with primary keyword

CALL TO ACTION:
Always end with a specific CTA: "Book a free workflow assessment" or "Get your cost estimate" or "Schedule a demo"
Never use generic "contact us" or "learn more".
