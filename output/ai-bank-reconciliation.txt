WHAT IS AI BANK RECONCILIATION
AI bank reconciliation is the automated process of matching bank statements with accounting records using artificial intelligence. Speedbots delivers a cloud‑based platform that pulls statements from banks, applies OCR and machine‑learning models, and generates accurate journal entries in minutes. The value proposition is a 90 % reduction in manual effort, a 5‑fold drop in errors, and real‑time visibility for finance leaders in Singapore’s fast‑paced market.
