WHAT IS BANK RECONCILIATION AUTOMATION
Bank reconciliation automation is a technology-driven process that matches a company’s bank statement entries with its internal accounting records automatically. For Malaysian finance managers, this means faster, more accurate reconciliations that comply with SST filing requirements. Speedbots delivers the solution through an AI‑powered engine that pulls data from banks, matches transactions, flags exceptions, and logs every step for audit purposes. By eliminating manual data entry and redundant checks, the tool shortens the reconciliation cycle from days to minutes.

THE HIDDEN COST OF MANUAL PROCESSES
- **Time waste**: A typical medium‑sized firm with 10 bank accounts spends 2–3 days (≈ 16 hours) each month reconciling. That is 4 weeks of labor for a single finance team.
- **Labor cost**: At a standard rate of RM 80 per hour, the monthly cost equals RM 2,400–RM 3,600 (30 hours).
- **Error rate**: Manual checks miss 5–8 % of entries. In a month of 6,000 transactions, this equates to 300–480 discrepancies that can go unnoticed until close.
- **Compliance risk**: Without automated tax coding, 10–15 % of SST‑related entries may be misclassified, resulting in penalties or audit adjustments.
- **Audit trail gaps**: Physical spreadsheets and email chains offer limited traceability. An audit can discover that 38 % of reconciliation steps lack a digital trail, raising compliance scores.

These hidden costs add up. If you multiply the labor cost by 12 months, you pay RM 28,800–RM 43,200 in recurring expenses, while the missed errors may cost RM 3,000–RM 5,000 annually in fines or corrective work. Speedbots’ automation reduces the time to under 30 minutes, cuts labor costs to below RM 600 per month, and brings error rates below 1 %. The net effect is up to a 75 % cost reduction and a payback period of only 1–2 months.

BEFORE AND AFTER: HOW AUTOMATION CHANGES YOUR WORKFLOW
**Workflow 1 – Bank Statement Import**
- *Before automation*: PDF statements are manually scanned in, amounts are typed into Excel, and then transferred into the ERP. The process consumes about 3 hours each month.
- *After automation*: Speedbots pulls statements via Maybank Open Banking APIs in real‑time. The system parses data, maps it to chart‑of‑account codes, and logs the transaction in the ERP within 5 minutes. Time saved: 75 % of the manual effort equals 2.25 hours per month.

**Workflow 2 – Transaction Matching**
- *Before automation*: Each transaction requires a clerical review in a spreadsheet. The process typically consumes 8 hours for 20 accounts.
- *After automation*: AI algorithms match 98 % of entries automatically. The user only reviews 2 % of 20 accounts, a manual effort of roughly 30 minutes. Time saved: 7 hours 30 minutes.

**Workflow 3 – Exception Handling & SST Coding**
- *Before automation*: Exceptions are mailed to auditors; SST codes are added manually during monthly reporting.
- *After automation*: Speedbots flags mismatches and auto‑assigns SST tax codes based on transaction description patterns, integrating directly into the LHDN e‑filing portal. Time saved: 1.5 hours each month, with a risk of tax coding errors cut by 90 %.

These workflow gains translate into an overall month‑over‑month average of 2 hours saved, a 30 % faster cycle, and a tangible improvement in working‑capital management.

HOW TO GET STARTED IN 6 STEPS
1. **Audit your current process** – List all bank accounts, reconciliation frequency, and software tools in use.
2. **Map your data flow** – Identify data sources (bank files, ERP systems), points where errors occur, and manual touchpoints.
3. **Evaluate connectivity options** – Check which banks support open banking APIs (Maybank, CIMB, RHB) and your ERP’s integration layer.
4. **Configure Speedbots** – Set up bank feeds, define matching rules, and assign the SST tax codes according to your accounting standards.
5. **Pilot with a subset of accounts** – Run parallel manual and automated reconciliations for 1–2 accounts to validate accuracy.
6. **Scale and monitor** – After a successful pilot, extend to all accounts, review the audit trail, and integrate the dashboard into your finance KPIs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
1. **Will Speedbots replace my ERP system?**
No. Speedbots is an overlay that connects to your existing ERP, pulling and pushing data without replacing core functions.

2. **What banks does it support in Malaysia?**
It supports all banks with open banking APIs: Maybank, CIMB, RHB, Bank Islam, and others. For others, PDF or electronic statement uploads are still available.

3. **How does it handle SST filing?**
The platform automatically tags each transaction with the correct ST1 or ST2 tax code and exports a ready‑to‑file XML for submission to LHDN’s e‑filing portal.

4. **What is the technical skill required for setup?**
A 3‑day technical workshop for your finance or IT team covers API key management, rule configuration, and dashboard setup.

5. **Can we run the system if the internet goes down?**
Reconciliation runs on the cloud, but you can manually import statements in an offline mode; the system will sync once connectivity is restored.

6. **Is data secure?**
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. The platform complies with ISO/IEC 27001 and local data residency regulations.
