Before automation, a bot start scenario begins with a paper or PDF invoice. An accountant scans it, re-types vendor, amount, and GST, then emails it for approval. The approver reviews a spreadsheet and the payment is scheduled by hand. This takes a full day per invoice.

After the Speedbots bot starts, the same invoice is captured by OCR in seconds. STAYING IN CONTROL: RISKS AND SAFEGUARDS
1. Data Accuracy – OCR misreads are possible. Mitigation: confidence thresholds and manual review queues.
2. Routing Errors – a wrong approver delays payment. Mitigation: rule-based routing plus fallback notifications.
3. Integration Failures – ERP or bank API downtime. Mitigation: retry schedules and a manual override queue.
4. Compliance Gaps – e-invoicing formats change. Mitigation: auto-filled e-invoice fields and deadline flags.
5. Staff Resistance – fear of job loss. Mitigation: communicate that bots handle repetitive work and free staff for analysis.
