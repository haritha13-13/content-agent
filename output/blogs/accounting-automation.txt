WHAT IS ACCOUNTING AUTOMATION
Accounting automation is the application of software tools that capture, validate, and process financial data with minimal human intervention. At Speedbots, we integrate OCR, AI‑based validation, and cloud workflows to replace manual entry, spreadsheet approvals, and paper filing. The result is a faster, more accurate ledger that frees finance teams to focus on analysis and strategy rather than routine tasks.

BEFORE AND AFTER: HOW AUTOMATION CHANGES YOUR WORKFLOW
**Invoice Capture & Validation**
Before: Physical mail or scanned PDFs are manually uploaded, data is entered into a spreadsheet, and each line item is cross‑checked against purchase orders. The cycle takes 4–5 days.
After: Scanned PDFs are fed into an OCR engine that extracts key fields. AI rules flag duplicate or missing entries instantly. The system auto‑populates the ledger and routes the invoice for approval in under 30 minutes. Time saved: 80 % of the original cycle.

**Bank Reconciliation**
Before: Bank statements are printed, manually matched line by line with ledger entries, and any discrepancies are highlighted in a separate sheet for investigation. The process lasts 3–4 days.
After: The bank feed is connected to the accounting system. AI matches receipts with payments, highlights mismatches, and suggests reconciliations. The entire task finishes in 1 day, with an audit trail for compliance. Time saved: 70 %.

**GST Compliance**
Before: Tax codes are entered manually into return forms, and the 64‑digit invoice number is extracted from paper receipts. GSTR‑1, GSTR‑2B, and GSTR‑3B are compiled in separate spreadsheets, taking 5–7 days.
After: The system extracts tax data from invoices, auto‑generates return entries, and e‑files them via GSTN API. The entire filing cycle drops to 1–2 days, eliminating late‑filing penalties.

These transformations illustrate how Speedbots turns a sluggish, error‑prone process into a streamlined, data‑driven workflow.

STAYING IN CONTROL: RISKS AND SAFEGUARDS
1. **Data Accuracy** – Risk: AI misreads data. Mitigation: implement a double‑check audit trail and manual override for flagged entries.
2. **Routing Errors** – Risk: approvals bypassed. Mitigation: enforce role‑based workflow rules and mandatory comments on rejections.
3. **Integration Failures** – Risk: bank feed disconnects. Mitigation: schedule regular sync checks and maintain a fallback manual reconciliation queue.
4. **Compliance Breaches** – Risk: incorrect GST filing. Mitigation: keep the system updated with the latest GSTN API changes and run quarterly compliance audits.
5. **Staff Resistance** – Risk: reluctance to adopt new tools. Mitigation: involve end‑users early, provide hands‑on training, and highlight time‑saving benefits.
