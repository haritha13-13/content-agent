STAYING IN CONTROL: RISKS AND SAFEGUARDS
- **Data accuracy**: AI may misinterpret ambiguous email text. Mitigation: Train the model with 500 sample emails and set a manual review threshold for 10 % of orders.
- **Routing errors**: Incorrect approver selection can delay approvals. Mitigation: Validate routing rules against a static approval matrix and run a dry‑run before go‑live.
- **Integration failures**: Connection to Tally or GST APIs may fail. Mitigation: Implement retry logic and real‑time monitoring dashboards.
- **Compliance gaps**: GST rates change quarterly. Mitigation: Subscribe to a local tax update service and auto‑refresh rate tables.
- **Staff resistance**: Employees fear job loss. Mitigation: Offer upskilling workshops and highlight how automation frees them for strategic tasks.
