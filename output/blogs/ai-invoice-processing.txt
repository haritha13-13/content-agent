FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Speedbots supports PDF, image, and scanned documents in English, and can be trained for multi‑language invoices.

2. How does Speedbots calculate GST and SST?
It applies Singapore tax rules: 7 % GST on services and goods, 8 % SST on specific categories, and auto‑generates tax line items per invoice.
