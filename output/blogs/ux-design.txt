WHAT IS UX DESIGN
UX design is the discipline of shaping digital experiences that feel intuitive and efficient for users. It blends research, visual design, and technology to create products that meet business goals while delighting customers. THE HIDDEN COST OF MANUAL PROCESSES
Manual UX workflows drain resources that could be invested in innovation. Consider the typical pain points in a mid‑size Malaysian tech firm:

- **User research cycles** – 5‑day loops that involve recruitment, testing, and analysis. Each cycle costs an average of RM 1 200 for a senior UX researcher, plus 40 % more time spent on data cleaning.
- **Design handoff** – Designers export PNGs, write spec sheets in Word, and email developers. The process takes 3 hrs per screen and produces 25 % more hand‑off defects.

These inefficiencies add up to an annual overhead of RM 120 000 for a team of five. STAYING IN CONTROL: RISKS AND SAFEGUARDS
1. **Data accuracy** – Risk: Incorrect user data feeds into design decisions.
Mitigation: Validate data sources, use encryption, conduct regular audits.
2. **Routing errors** – Risk: Automated approvals bypass required stakeholders.
Mitigation: Implement strict workflow rules and audit logs.
3. **Integration failures** – Risk: API disconnects between Figma and Jira.
Mitigation: Use webhook monitoring, fallback manual processes, and versioned APIs.
4. **Compliance** – Risk: Non‑compliance with SST or data protection laws.
Mitigation: Include tax fields in all invoices, encrypt personal data, comply with PDPA.
5. **Staff resistance** – Risk: Teams avoid new tools.
Mitigation: Provide hands‑on training, showcase quick wins, involve champions.
