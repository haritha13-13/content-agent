STAYING IN CONTROL: RISKS AND SAFEGUARDS
1. Data Accuracy – Risk: OCR misreads can propagate errors. Mitigation: Use confidence thresholds and manual verification for low‑confidence entries.
2. Routing Errors – Risk: Mis‑routed invoices delay payments. Mitigation: Build strict approval matrices and audit logs.
3. Integration Failures – Risk: ERP sync issues cause duplicate entries. Mitigation: Schedule regular health checks and use API versioning.
4. Compliance – Risk: GST mis‑calculations lead to penalties. Mitigation: Keep GST rules updated via Speedbots compliance module.
5. Staff Resistance – Risk: Employees fear job loss. Mitigation: Communicate that automation frees them for higher‑value tasks and provide training.
