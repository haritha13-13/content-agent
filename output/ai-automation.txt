STAYING IN CONTROL: RISKS AND SAFEGUARDS
1. **Data accuracy** – Risk: OCR misreads. Mitigation: Use multi‑layer validation and human review checkpoints.
2. **Routing errors** – Risk: Invoices routed to wrong department. Mitigation: Implement rule‑based routing with audit logs.
3. **Integration failures** – Risk: ERP sync issues. Mitigation: Conduct API compatibility testing and schedule regular health checks.
4. **Compliance gaps** – Risk: GST code updates missed. Mitigation: Subscribe to GST update feeds and auto‑refresh tax tables quarterly.
5. **Staff resistance** – Risk: Employees fear job loss. Mitigation: Provide training that shows automation as a productivity enhancer, not a replacement.
