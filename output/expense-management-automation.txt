WHAT IS EXPENSE MANAGEMENT AUTOMATION
Expense management automation is a technology‑driven system that captures, validates, routes, and reconciles employee expense claims without manual intervention. By integrating OCR, AI policy engines, and bank feed APIs, Speedbots turns a paper‑filled cycle into a frictionless digital workflow. The result is faster approvals, fewer errors, and real‑time insight into spending – all while keeping compliance with Malaysia’s SST and IRBM requirements.

THE HIDDEN COST OF MANUAL PROCESSES
Manual expense handling drains resources, inflates costs, and exposes firms to compliance risks. Key pain points include:

- **Long approval cycles** – 5–7 days on average for a single report, driven by email chains and manual sign‑offs.
- **High error rates** – about 15 % of approvals contain mistakes such as duplicate entries or policy violations.
- **Receipt mismanagement** – 25 % of reports are rejected due to missing or unreadable receipts, causing re‑submission delays of 10–15 days.
- **Time spent on reconciliation** – Finance teams spend 2 hrs per batch for a 500‑entry month, leading to 40 hrs of manual work each month.

These inefficiencies translate into tangible costs. A mid‑size firm with 120 employees can see annual expense processing costs climb to RM 250,000, while productivity losses amount to RM 50,000 per year. Speedbots addresses every bottleneck with a single platform that automates capture, policy validation, routing, reconciliation, and reporting.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
1. **What data does Speedbots collect from receipts?**
The system extracts only necessary fields – date, amount, vendor, tax code, and category. No personal data is stored beyond what is needed for compliance.

2. **Can Speedbots enforce Malaysia’s SST thresholds?**
Yes. The policy engine includes pre‑built templates for 5 % and 6 % SST rates and automatically flags claims that exceed limits.

3. **How secure is the integration with my ERP?**
All connections use TLS 1.2+, OAuth 2.0 authentication, and audit logs. Data is never stored on Speedbots servers beyond the retention period defined in your contract.

6. **Is there a mobile app for employees?**
